# Place coverage review — 19 October 2026

## Metro, service locations and professional directories

The next source wave expands the 49,159-object publication to **49,533 mapped
objects: 374 additions, zero removed IDs**. It accepts 106 official service/chain
records (76 additions, 30 existing enrichments) and 310 public Yell business
directory records (298 additions, 12 existing enrichments). The two ledgers retain
40 and 76 unresolved candidates respectively. No city coverage percentage is
claimed. Public directory presence does not prove current operation.

The official service wave includes Tegeta, Amboli, Greenway, USA2Georgia,
Inex, Onex, Silknet and selected insurance/partner locations. It adds 28 reviewed
source photos and 10 official logos. The professional wave covers accountants,
lawyers, notaries, consulting offices, libraries and neutral casino location
facts. Its 191 website links, 308 public office phone records and 168 source hour
records retain their source attribution. A check of 195 linked website entries
corroborated 54 visible official addresses, corrected four exact points and held
three conflicting new candidates. Shared notary-chamber pages do not verify an
individual office. Seven official logos cover eight exact professional pages;
FINO and AACC share an explicitly labelled photograph of their actual building.
Personal tax identifiers, reviews and marketing prose were not copied.

Metro symbols now use **48 documented entrances across 23 stations**, named by
explicit OSM stop-area membership. Station-center records remain available at
their original IDs but are excluded from map symbols/search. Older map links
redirect to a real entrance. Nadzaladevi's entrance is `osm_node_1452387939`
at `41.7326229, 44.7970003`.

The audit also found **23 legacy GeoHub station pages** with seeded station
coordinates, including a misplaced Nadzaladevi record. Their exact station
identities are now reviewed source links. Display names and station coordinates
are corrected; old coordinates/names remain in provenance, IDs/ownership/activity
remain intact, and map destinations use the entrance. The native display bridge
now has 44 links to 43 distinct source records. No name-proximity rule is used to
assign these identities.

Actual public menu/product files and official service files are now attached to
canonical places. `place-content-audit.json` records current nonempty catalog,
photo/logo and editorial coverage by category. Large marketplace shops remain
explicitly partial until every category page is fetched. Product/source prices
are dated, expire, and are not asserted to be in-store prices or stock. Unknown
prices remain unknown. Source dietary flags are retained only when explicit.

The 3,551 accepted marketplace venues have all been attempted: 2,849 source
catalogs are complete, 701 are partial, and one source returns HTTP 404. The
snapshot contains 206,670 source items, 201,583 with source image URLs. Eleven
full responses are empty; they are not fabricated menus. Official sources add
245 service entries tied to 141 exact places. These are dated source snapshots,
not assertions of current stock, in-store prices or all-city completeness.

## Earlier public-directory expansion

Compared with the previously published 45,637-object snapshot, the current
publication contains **49,159 mapped objects: 3,522 additions and zero removed
previous IDs**. Another **2,604 previously published places** received reviewed
source enrichment. These are location records, not a count of unique companies
or a claim that every Tbilisi location has been found.

The accepted source inventory contains 6,356 rows: 2,805 official institutional,
chain, mall and service-directory rows, plus 3,551 public Wolt merchant listings.
It reconciles Nikora, SPAR, Pharmadepot, Liberty, Magti, Cellfie, 2 Nabiji, Libre,
Fresco, Europroduct, TBC, Basisbank, Credo, PSP, Aversi, Georgia Post, EMIS, EQE,
municipal libraries/museums, four mall directories and selected clinic sources.
Rows can enrich the same canonical place, so source counts are not added together
to produce a city completeness percentage.

- 4,090 accepted source rows have their own source-associated photograph or
  listing illustration; 3,149 distinct local photo files preserve provenance.
- 1,611 new logo assets bring the registry to 1,820 reviewed artworks. Brand
  resolution identifies 9,326 mapped objects, including branches and equipment.
- 720 source-backed mall tenants are reconciled with existing mapped tenants;
  11 bilingual duplicates were merged. The combined mall directories contain
  1,158 links across 14 of the 15 mapped malls. 491 source floor labels are kept.
- 238 school rows, 42 university rows, 8 municipal library rows and 8 museum rows
  were accepted from the reviewed institutional directories. These do not prove
  full coverage of every campus, classroom, public/private school or library.
- PSP and Aversi contribute 266 accepted rows (220 source matches, 46 additions)
  and 225 reviewed real branch photographs. The source inventory accounts for
  all 344 reviewed candidates and retains 78 ambiguous rows outside publication.
- Wolt's 4,009 observed listings yield 3,551 accepted rows (1,091 source matches,
  2,460 additions); 458 unresolved, virtual or conflicting candidates remain
  withheld. Listing visibility depends on the queried delivery areas. Images
  of dishes/products are explicitly labelled as listing illustrations.

Source-backed editorial content is labelled **GeoHub directory**; it does not
pretend to be a merchant-authored announcement or a customer review. No ratings,
stock, discounts, preparation times or opening hours were inferred from Wolt.
Publisher media is not assigned an invented open license or capture date.

Known gaps remain explicit in `source-directory-institutions.audit.json`,
`source-directory-food.audit.json` and `source-audits/`: 51 unresolved school
rows, 6 municipal library branches with unresolved address geometry, disputed
clinic coordinates, ambiguous chain/ATM/pharmacy matches, unavailable source
photos and GPC's empty locator response. These are reasons for further review,
not evidence that the locations do not exist. OSM is still the primary broad
source for infrastructure, transport, offices, services and street furniture.

Validation checks canonical IDs, parent/tenant links, taxonomy, photo/logo hashes,
source URLs and preservation of native owner fields. Desktop/mobile browser checks
cover source-photo pages, school/chain/hospital logos, mall-to-tenant navigation,
map search and existing real-offer links. Earlier published hashed assets are
retained for already-open tabs; unpublished intermediate builds are not shipped.

## Source limitations and unresolved work

- A source snapshot is not a complete city directory. There is no defensible
  “100% of Tbilisi” denominator. A directory scrape or record total cannot prove
  every shop, tenant, office, entrance and institution has been captured.
- The map list previously stopped at 80 results. This was a display limit,
  not the source's total; it must not be presented as the total city coverage.
- Libraries were already imported (40 records); the review adds four documented
  indoor collections. Municipal directories and the national education register
  are useful cross-checks but may list institutions rather than every branch.
- International University of Tbilisi lists 35 Ksani Street, and East West
  University lists an additional Uznadze campus. Reliable point/address geometry
  was not established in this pass; neither was assigned a guessed street-center
  coordinate. They remain candidates for a verified follow-up import.
- The mapped Mediathek `osm_way_1330515311` explicitly reports reconstruction
  and disuse. Its location remains available with an inactive-source flag;
  historical opening hours are not exposed as current hours. Former School 128
  is an educational site, not an asserted active school. The former Mikeladze
  library and residential developments with “University” names were not promoted
  to active libraries/universities.
- The existing health count is unchanged. This pass did not establish that every
  clinic, pharmacy or doctor's office in the city is present. A source-backed
  institution/branch inventory is required before claiming completeness.
- Public building/land-use geometry does not prove current tenants or operation.
  Mall lists derived from mapped tenant points remain partial. Historical media
  and brand logos must not be presented as current site-specific photographs.

## Reproduce the comparison

Retain the baseline manifest and its content-hashed shards, then run:

```powershell
node web/tools/audit-place-coverage.mjs --baseline path/to/baseline-manifest.json
node web/tools/generate-native-place-source-records.mjs
node --test web/tools/import-tbilisi.test.mjs web/tools/coordinates.test.mjs
```

The JSON audit lists category/group totals, all named additions, source facts,
removed IDs and coordinate corrections. The build manifest additionally records
OSM versus official additions, source URLs and mapped-building counts.
